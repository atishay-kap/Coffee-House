about us why choose us

what makes Garden House Café special!

Cozy establishment located in the heart of South Extension & Shahpur Jat, Panchsheel Park. Known for fresh all-day breakfast, fluffy pancakes, signature acai bowls, wholesome salads, and artisan beverages in an aesthetic garden setting.

Whether you're looking for a relaxing morning coffee, a tranquil workspace with books and chilled music, or a health-focused meal with friends, we offer an inviting ambience with warm hospitality.
